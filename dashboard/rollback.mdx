---
title: "Instant rollback"
description: "Revert your live docs to an earlier successful build without touching Git."
---

<Callout type="info">
  Instant rollback is available on the [Enterprise plan](/docs/dashboard/billing).
  Only workspace owners and admins can trigger or reverse a rollback.
</Callout>

Instant rollback lets you switch the live version of your documentation to any
previous successful build — immediately, without a Git revert or a new push. Use
it when a bad deployment goes live and you need to restore a working version
while you investigate.

## How rollback works

When you roll back, Velu swaps the live build to the selected earlier deployment.
The change takes effect immediately. Git history is untouched, and new commits
continue to build in the background — they just don't go live while a rollback
is active.

Your site enters a **rollback state** that stays visible in the dashboard. While
in rollback state:

- A banner shows on the Overview page noting that an earlier version is live.
- New successful builds queue up but are not promoted to production automatically.
- You can roll back further to an even older build if one is available.
- When you're ready to restore the latest build, click **Promote latest**.

## Roll back a deployment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dashboard Overview">
    Go to [docs.getvelu.com](https://docs.getvelu.com) and open your site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Instant Rollback">
    In the **Production Deployment** card at the top of the page, click
    **Instant Rollback**. The button is disabled if no earlier successful build
    is available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the builds">
    A dialog shows the current live build and the previous successful build you
    will roll back to — commit message, short SHA, author, and deploy time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a reason (optional)">
    Enter a short note explaining why you are rolling back. This appears in
    **Activity → Changes** so your team has context. Leave it blank to skip.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Continue**, review the warning, then click **Roll back now**. The
    previous build goes live immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
  Readers see the previous deployment as soon as you confirm. The change is
  immediate — there is no staging step.
</Callout>

## Promote the latest build

Once the issue that caused the rollback is fixed, promote the latest successful
build to restore normal publishing.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the rollback banner">
    The Overview page shows a yellow banner: "You're serving an earlier version
    of your docs." This banner stays until you promote or new builds catch up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Promote latest">
    Click **Promote latest** in the banner. Velu immediately makes the most
    recent successful build live and exits rollback state. Normal auto-promotion
    resumes for future builds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Rollback status in Activity

Every rollback and promotion appears as a row in the **Activity** table on the
Overview page:

| Trigger | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `rollback` | You rolled back to an earlier build. The reason you entered (if any) appears in the Changes column. |
| `promote` | You promoted the latest successful build after a rollback. |

These rows show a **Completed** status badge rather than the normal build
statuses.

## Who can roll back

Only workspace owners and admins with the `can_manage_rollbacks` permission can
use the Instant Rollback and Promote latest controls. Members without this
permission do not see the button.
