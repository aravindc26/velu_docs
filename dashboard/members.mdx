Manage who can work on your site under **Settings › Members** at
`/settings/members`. You invite people by email, and they get access once they
accept.

<Callout type="info">
  Only the site owner can see and manage members. Members don't have access to
  the Members page.
</Callout>

## Roles

There are two roles:

- **Owner**: full access, including members, billing, and domain settings. Each
  site has one owner.
- **Member**: can work on content and configuration, but can't manage the team,
  billing, or domains.

## Invite a teammate

Enter the person's name and email and send the invite. They receive an invite
link that expires after 7 days. Once they accept and sign in, they appear in the
member list.

## Manage members

From the member list you can:

- **Resend invite**: send the invite email again if it expired or was missed.
- **Reactivate**: restore access for a member you previously revoked.
- **Revoke**: remove a member's access. Revoking signs them out immediately.
  Any content they created stays in the site.
