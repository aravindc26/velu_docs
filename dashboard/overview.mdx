The dashboard at [docs.getvelu.com](https://docs.getvelu.com) is where you create
a docs site, publish it, and manage its domain, team, and billing. It works
whether you write in the browser or keep your docs in GitHub.

<Image src="/docs/images/dashboard/overview.png" alt="The Velu dashboard overview page: a site preview, the Live status, Rebuild and Visit site buttons, the domain, and the Activity table" />

## Sign up and create your first site

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an account">
    Sign up with your email and a password. Passwords need at least 8
    characters, one special character, and both upper- and lower-case letters.
    There's no social login.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Velu sends a verification link to your address. Open it to activate your
    account: the link is valid for 24 hours. You have to verify before you can
    sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how you'll write">
    On first sign-in, pick one of two modes:

    - **Editor-only (managed)**: Velu hosts your content and you write entirely
      in the web editor. No GitHub, no local setup. Velu seeds a starter site
      and builds it right away.
    - **Docs-as-code + editor**: install the Velu GitHub App. Velu creates a
      repository from the docs template and builds it. You can still use the web
      editor alongside your local workflow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your site">
    Every site gets a free subdomain like `bright-panda.getvelu.com`. Your first
    build starts automatically, and the Overview page shows its progress.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  You can switch to a custom domain later under **Settings › Domain setup**. See
  [Custom domains](/docs/deploy/custom-domains) for DNS setup.
</Callout>

## Describe what you're building

Before Velu builds your first site, you can tell it what you're building so it
can write a first draft. At the top of the page, choose the type of site:

- **Docs**: product or developer documentation.
- **Help center**: answers to the questions your customers ask. Describe your
  product and those questions, or paste your website or current help center URL.

Then describe your product and what the site should cover, or paste a website
URL. You can also give Velu more material to work from:

- **Add GitHub repo**: choose up to five repositories as reference material.
  Velu doesn't modify them. It creates a separate private repository for your
  docs in your connected GitHub account.
- **Upload file**: attach up to five `.pdf`, `.docx`, `.md`, or `.txt` files.

Click **Generate and continue** (**Generate help center and continue** for a help
center). If you'd rather start from a blank site, click **Skip, start with a
template**.

## Claiming a site built for you

Velu can build and deploy a docs site for you, for example when it migrates
your existing docs. In that case you receive a signup link from the person who
set it up, and the site is already live when you open it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your signup link">
    The page shows your company name and the address of your site. Open the
    address to check the site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your work email">
    Enter the email address you want to use for your account and click
    **Continue**. This is the email your account uses from then on, so it
    doesn't have to match the address the link was sent to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Velu sends a verification link to that address. It's valid for 24 hours. If
    the email doesn't arrive, click **Resend email**. If you typed the wrong
    address, click **Change it**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your password">
    Open the verification link and set a password. You then land in your
    dashboard with the site already in your workspace. There's no workspace
    setup step.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the email you enter already has a Velu account, the page tells you. Log in
instead, or use a different email.

<Callout type="warning">
  A signup link works once and for a limited time. If the page says the link has
  already been used or has expired, log in if you already set up your account.
  Otherwise ask the person who sent it for a new one. Creating a new link
  replaces the previous one.
</Callout>

## Signing up with an enterprise invitation

If your company has an Enterprise agreement with Velu, you may receive an
invitation link rather than signing up through the normal flow. The invitation
link is specific to your email address and sets your workspace up on the
Enterprise plan automatically.

When you open an invitation link:

- The signup page shows a banner confirming your invitation and the email
  address it was issued for.
- Your email is pre-filled and locked to the invited address. You cannot change
  it.
- Enter a password and your workspace name to complete signup. No email
  verification step is required — the invitation replaces it.

<Callout type="warning">
  Invitation links are single-use and expire after 14 days. If your link has
  already been used or has expired, contact the person who sent it to request a
  new one.
</Callout>

Once you complete signup your workspace is on the Enterprise plan with no
additional billing steps needed.

## The Overview page

The home page of the dashboard shows a live preview of your site with its
current status:

- **Live**: the site is deployed and serving.
- **Building**: a deployment is in progress. The status updates on its own as
  the build runs.

From here you can:

- **Rebuild**: trigger a fresh deployment.
- **Visit site**: open the published site in a new tab.

Below the buttons you'll see your site's domain, and (in docs-as-code mode),
the connected repository and branch. The **Activity** table lists recent
deployments with their status and time. See [Deployments](/docs/dashboard/deployments)
for what each status means.

## Navigation

The left sidebar holds everything you manage:

| Item | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | The Overview page: site preview, status, and deployment activity. |
| **Editor** | The [web editor](/docs/dashboard/editor) for pages and config. |
| **Feedback** | Reader "Was this helpful?" results and Ask AI answers. |
| **Agents › Workflows** | AI automations that open pull requests. See [Workflows](/docs/dashboard/workflows). |
| **Agents › Guide** | Set up an in-app assistant for your users. See [Guide](/docs/dashboard/guide). |
| **Settings › Domain setup** | Add and verify a [custom domain](/docs/deploy/custom-domains). |
| **Settings › API keys** | Create keys for the [REST API](/docs/api-reference/introduction). |
| **Settings › MCP** | Connect AI tools to your docs via the [Admin MCP server](/docs/dashboard/mcp-settings). |
| **Settings › Members** | Invite and manage your [team](/docs/dashboard/members). |
| **Settings › Billing** | Manage your [plan and credits](/docs/dashboard/billing). |
