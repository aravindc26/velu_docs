title: "Billing & credits"
description: "Choose a plan and manage the credits that power Ask AI and Workflows."
---

Manage your plan and credits under **Settings › Billing** at `/settings/billing`.
Credits pay for AI features: Ask AI replies and Workflow runs. Only the site
owner can access billing.

## Plans

<Image src="/docs/images/dashboard/billing.png" alt="The billing page plan picker showing Free, Begin, Value, and Enterprise plans with prices and features" />

| Plan | Price | Monthly credits | Ask AI | Workflows |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | $0 | - | No | No |
| **Begin** | $9 / mo | 300 | Yes | No |
| **Value** | $49 / mo | 1,500 | Yes | Yes |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Custom | Yes | Yes |

Every plan includes a hosted docs site, a custom domain, and Markdown and
`llms.txt` export. **Begin** adds [Ask AI](/docs/ai/ask-ai), the chat assistant on
your published site. **Value** adds [Workflows](/docs/dashboard/workflows), AI
automations that open pull requests. Choose monthly or annual billing: annual
gives you two months free.

**Enterprise** is built for growing documentation teams that need advanced controls.
It includes everything in Value, plus [instant rollback](/docs/dashboard/rollback),
[shared session personalization](/docs/writing/personalization), and a custom
credit allowance. Pricing is custom — click **Contact sales** on the billing page
or email sales to get started.

[Private documentation](/docs/deploy/authentication-setup) password protection is
available on all plans. Workspace authentication needs Begin or higher. OAuth and
JWT authentication require an Enterprise plan.
[Preview deployments](/docs/deploy/preview-deployments) require a Begin or
Value plan. [Shared session personalization](/docs/writing/personalization)
and [instant rollback](/docs/dashboard/rollback) require an Enterprise plan.

## Credits

Credits are the unit Velu charges AI usage against. One credit is worth $0.01.
Your plan includes a monthly allotment, and usage draws it down:

| Action | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Ask AI reply | 1 credit |
| Workflow run | 14 credits |

Begin includes 300 credits a month and Value includes 1,500. When you need more,
buy credits from the Billing page. You can also turn on **auto-top-up** to buy
credits automatically when your balance runs low, so Ask AI and Workflows keep
running.

<Callout type="note">
  Included credits refresh each month with your plan. Credits you buy on top are
  used when the included allotment runs out. Enterprise plans have a custom credit
  allowance — manual top-up and auto-top-up are not available on Enterprise.
</Callout>
