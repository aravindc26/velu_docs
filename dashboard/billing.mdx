title: "Billing & credits"
description: "Choose a plan and manage the credits that power Ask AI and Workflows."
---

Manage your plan and credits under **Settings › Billing** at `/settings/billing`.
Credits pay for AI features: Ask AI replies and Workflow runs. Only the site
owner can access billing.

## Plans

<Image src="/docs/images/dashboard/billing.png" alt="The billing page plan picker showing Free, Begin, and Value plans with prices and features" />

| Plan | Price | Monthly credits | Ask AI | Workflows | Private auth methods |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | $0 | - | No | No | Password |
| **Begin** | $9 / mo | 3,000 | Yes | No | Password, Workspace |
| **Value** | $49 / mo | 15,000 | Yes | Yes | Password, Workspace |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Custom | Yes | Yes | Password, Workspace, OAuth 2.0, JWT |

Every plan includes a hosted docs site, a custom domain, and Markdown and
`llms.txt` export. **Begin** adds [Ask AI](/docs/ai/ask-ai), the chat assistant on
your published site. **Value** adds [Workflows](/docs/dashboard/workflows), AI
automations that open pull requests. **Value** also includes
[shared session personalization](/docs/writing/personalization). Choose monthly
or annual billing: annual gives you two months free.

The [authentication settings UI](/docs/deploy/authentication-setup) only shows the
methods your plan supports — if an option is not visible, your plan does not
include it. Password protection is available on all plans. Workspace authentication
requires Begin or higher. OAuth 2.0 and JWT require an Enterprise plan.
[Preview deployments](/docs/deploy/preview-deployments) require a Begin or
Value plan. [Shared session personalization](/docs/writing/personalization)
requires a Value plan.

## Credits

Credits are the unit Velu charges AI usage against. One credit is worth $0.001,
so $1 buys 1,000 credits. Your plan includes a monthly allotment, and usage
draws it down. Velu charges the actual cost of the AI work with no markup:

| Action | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Ask AI reply | The cost of the reply, at least 1 credit. A reply that fails is free. |
| Workflow run | The cost of the run. Only completed runs are charged. |

Begin includes 3,000 credits a month and Value includes 15,000. New workspaces
start with a 14-day Value trial that includes 15,000 credits, Ask AI, and
Workflows. A banner shows how much of the trial is left. When the trial ends,
your workspace moves to Free unless you choose a paid plan. Credits you bought
are kept.

### Workflow run costs

A workflow run needs a balance of at least 140 credits to start. When a run
completes, its credits used appear in the **Credits** column on the
[Runs tab](/docs/dashboard/workflows#the-workflows-and-runs-tabs). Skipped and
failed runs show a dash because they cost nothing. A single run is never charged
more than 1,400 credits.

### Buy credits

Choose **Buy credits** on the Billing page to top up your balance:

- The smallest top-up is 5,000 credits ($5). The dialog shows the total before
  you continue to checkout.
- You can buy up to 1,000,000 credits ($1,000) at a time. For a larger purchase,
  the dialog offers a link to contact sales for volume pricing.
- Credits you buy never expire.

You can also turn on **auto-top-up** to buy credits automatically when your
balance runs low, so Ask AI and Workflows keep running. Set the balance that
triggers a top-up and the amount to buy. Each automatic top-up follows the same
5,000 to 1,000,000 credit range.

<Callout type="note">
  Included credits refresh each month with your plan. Credits you buy on top are
  used when the included allotment runs out.
</Callout>
